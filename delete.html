GETHU Account Deletion

Last updated: August 20, 2026

You can request deletion of your GETHU account and associated personal information by contacting GETHU support.

How to Request Deletion

  1. Send an email to support@gethu.app.
  2. Use the subject line GETHU Account Deletion Request.
  3. Provide the information needed to identify your account, such as the registered mobile number or email address.
Security: We may need to verify account ownership before processing a deletion request.

What Happens Next

After verification, we will process the request in accordance with applicable law, our retention requirements, and our legitimate operational and security needs.

Important

Some information may need to be retained where required by law, necessary for fraud or security prevention, required to resolve disputes, or otherwise permitted by applicable law.

Privacy

For more information about retention and deletion, see the Privacy Policy.
