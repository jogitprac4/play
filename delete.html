Account Deletion

If you want to delete your GETHU account, you can submit an account deletion request by email.

Last updated: August 21, 2026
Account Deletion Request
support@gethu.app

Tap the email address to open your device's email application and send your deletion request to GETHU support.

1. How to Request Account Deletion

The only way to request deletion of a GETHU account is by sending an email to:

support@gethu.app

1
Send an email
Send your request to support@gethu.app.
2
State your request
Clearly state that you want your GETHU account to be deleted.
3
Account verification
We may need to verify that the request is associated with the account owner before processing the request.
4
Request processing
After verification, GETHU will process the account deletion request according to applicable requirements.

2. What Happens When Your Account Is Deleted?

When an account deletion request is approved and processed, GETHU will take reasonable steps to delete or de-identify information associated with the account that is eligible for deletion.

This may include account and profile information, as well as user content and other information associated with the account, subject to applicable retention requirements.

Some information may be retained

Certain information may need to be retained where reasonably necessary for legal obligations, fraud prevention, security, dispute resolution, enforcement of agreements, or other purposes permitted by applicable law.

3. After Account Deletion

Once the deletion process has been completed, you may no longer be able to access the deleted GETHU account or information associated with it.

Deleting an account should therefore be considered a permanent action for the information that is eligible for deletion.

4. Account Verification

To protect users from unauthorized deletion requests, GETHU may need to verify that a deletion request has been made by the account owner or an appropriately authorized person.

We may request reasonable information needed to establish ownership of the account before processing the request.

5. Information We May Need to Retain

Account deletion does not necessarily require the immediate removal of every record associated with an account.

GETHU may retain limited information where reasonably necessary to:

  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.
  • Prevent or investigate fraud, abuse, or security incidents.
  • Resolve disputes.
  • Establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.
  • Protect the security and integrity of GETHU.

Retained information will be handled according to applicable legal and security requirements.

6. Privacy and Data Protection

For additional information about how GETHU collects, uses, protects, and retains information, please review our Privacy Policy and Data Protection information.

7. Questions About Account Deletion

If you have questions about deleting your GETHU account or want to submit a deletion request, contact:

support@gethu.app

This email address is the only contact method provided by GETHU for account deletion requests.
