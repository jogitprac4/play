GETHU SECURITY

Security

GETHU uses reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect accounts, information, and the systems used to provide the GETHU service.

Last updated: August 21, 2026
Our security approach

Security is an ongoing part of how we build and operate GETHU. We use access controls, secure communications, monitoring, and other safeguards appropriate to the systems and information involved.

1. Account Security

GETHU uses authentication and authorization mechanisms designed to help protect user accounts and prevent unauthorized access.

Depending on the functionality being accessed, security controls may include authentication credentials, access tokens, permissions, and server-side authorization checks.

Authentication

Access to protected GETHU functionality requires appropriate authentication.

Authorization

Authenticated users are subject to authorization controls designed to limit access to permitted resources and actions.

Account protection

Security mechanisms are used to help protect accounts from unauthorized access and misuse.

2. Data Security

GETHU uses reasonable safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, loss, or misuse.

Depending on the system and type of information, safeguards may include:

  • Secure transmission of information where appropriate.
  • Authentication and authorization controls.
  • Access restrictions for protected resources.
  • Security logging and monitoring.
  • Infrastructure and application security controls.
  • Measures intended to detect and respond to security incidents.

3. Secure Communication

Information exchanged between the GETHU application and our services may be transmitted using secure communication mechanisms appropriate to the service and information involved.

GETHU also uses security controls around authenticated service communication and protected application endpoints where appropriate.

4. Access Controls

Access to systems and information is intended to be limited according to legitimate operational requirements.

Depending on the system, access may be controlled through authentication, authorization, permissions, service-level controls, and other security mechanisms.

We seek to avoid unnecessary access to user information and maintain controls appropriate to the systems involved.

5. Monitoring and Security Operations

GETHU may use logs, monitoring, and other technical mechanisms to help maintain the security, availability, and reliability of the platform.

Security-related information may be used to investigate suspicious activity, unauthorized access, service abuse, fraud, or other incidents that could affect GETHU or its users.

6. Security Incidents

GETHU maintains processes intended to identify, investigate, contain, and respond to security incidents affecting the service.

When appropriate, we may take steps to protect affected systems, investigate the cause of an incident, reduce its impact, and implement corrective measures.

Where required by applicable law, GETHU may also provide appropriate notifications regarding qualifying security incidents.

7. Third-Party Service Providers

GETHU may rely on third-party providers for infrastructure and technical services required to operate the application.

These services may include hosting, storage, authentication, notifications, communications, media processing, security, and other technical infrastructure.

Where third parties process information on behalf of GETHU, we seek to use providers appropriate to the services they provide and the information they handle.

8. Audio and Video Calling

GETHU may provide real-time audio and video calling functionality.

Calling functionality may require technical information to establish, maintain, secure, and terminate a connection between participants.

GETHU applies appropriate security controls to the systems used to provide calling functionality.

9. Protecting Your Account

Users also play an important role in keeping their accounts secure.

  • Keep your account credentials and authentication information private.
  • Do not knowingly share access to your account with other people.
  • Keep your device and operating system updated.
  • Use appropriate security protections on the device used to access GETHU.
  • Contact GETHU if you believe your account has been accessed without authorization.

10. Important Security Limitation

No system can guarantee absolute security

Although GETHU uses reasonable security measures, no method of electronic transmission, storage, or internet communication can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

We therefore cannot guarantee that information will never be accessed, disclosed, altered, or destroyed as a result of circumstances beyond our reasonable control.

11. Report a Security Concern

If you believe you have discovered a security issue involving GETHU, please report it to:

GETHU Security

Send security-related concerns to:

support@gethu.app

When reporting a security concern, please provide enough information for us to understand and investigate the issue.

12. Data Protection

Security is one part of GETHU's broader approach to protecting information.

For additional information about how GETHU handles, protects, and retains information, see our Data Protection page and Privacy Policy .

13. Changes to This Security Information

GETHU may update this page as our security practices, systems, services, or applicable requirements change.

When we make changes, we will update the Last updated date shown at the top of this page.
