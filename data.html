Data Protection

GETHU takes the protection of user information seriously. This page explains the principles and practices we use to protect, manage, retain, and handle information used to operate GETHU.

Last updated: August 21, 2026
Our approach to data protection

We collect and process information that is reasonably necessary to provide and secure GETHU.

We use technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for legitimate service, security, legal, and operational purposes.

1. Our Data Protection Principles

GETHU follows a set of principles when handling information associated with our users and services.

Purpose

Information is handled for legitimate purposes connected with providing, securing, and improving GETHU.

Minimization

We seek to collect and process information that is reasonably relevant to the services and functionality being provided.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, loss, or disclosure.

Retention

Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary for service, security, legal, and operational purposes.

Responsible Access

Access to information is limited according to operational requirements and appropriate authorization.

2. Information We Protect

Depending on how you use GETHU, the information we handle may include:

  • Account and authentication information.
  • Profile information and profile content.
  • Photos, videos, and other content you choose to upload.
  • Information relating to follows, requests, and other social activity.
  • Technical information required to operate and secure the service.
  • Information required to support audio and video calling.
  • Notification and device-related identifiers used to deliver service notifications.

For more information about the categories of information GETHU collects and how they are used, see our Privacy Policy .

3. Protecting Information

GETHU uses reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information throughout the systems used to operate the service.

Depending on the system and type of information, safeguards may include:

  • Authentication and access controls.
  • Authorization controls for protected resources.
  • Encryption or secure transmission mechanisms where appropriate.
  • Monitoring and logging of relevant system activity.
  • Security controls around application infrastructure and services.
  • Measures designed to identify and respond to security incidents.
Security is an ongoing process

No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security. GETHU continuously works to maintain and improve the protections used to safeguard the service and its users.

4. Access to Information

Access to protected information is intended to be limited to authorized systems, personnel, or service providers that require access for legitimate operational purposes.

Access may be controlled through authentication, authorization, service-level permissions, and other security mechanisms appropriate to the system.

We seek to avoid unnecessary access to user information and review access controls as part of our ongoing security practices.

5. Service Providers

GETHU may use third-party providers to support infrastructure and features required to operate the service.

Depending on the service provided, these providers may support areas such as:

  • Hosting and infrastructure.
  • Data and media storage.
  • Authentication and security.
  • Notifications and communications.
  • Media processing.
  • Other technical services required by GETHU.

Service providers are expected to handle information only as necessary to provide the services for which they are engaged and according to applicable contractual, legal, and security requirements.

6. Data Retention

GETHU does not retain information indefinitely without a legitimate reason.

Information may be retained for periods reasonably necessary to:

  • Provide and maintain the GETHU service.
  • Maintain account and transaction records where applicable.
  • Protect the security and integrity of the platform.
  • Investigate abuse, fraud, or security incidents.
  • Resolve disputes and enforce applicable agreements.
  • Meet legal or regulatory obligations.

When information is no longer reasonably required, it may be deleted, anonymized, or otherwise handled in accordance with applicable requirements and operational practices.

7. Account Deletion

Users may request deletion of their GETHU account and applicable personal information.

Account deletion may involve removing or de-identifying information associated with the account, subject to information that GETHU may need to retain for legitimate legal, security, fraud-prevention, or other permitted purposes.

To learn how to request account deletion, visit our Account Deletion page.

8. Data Transmission

Information may be transmitted between your device and GETHU systems when you use the application.

GETHU uses appropriate security mechanisms for protected communications where applicable.

Because no internet transmission method is completely risk-free, users should also protect their devices, account credentials, and access information.

9. Your Role in Protecting Your Account

Data protection is a shared responsibility.

Users should:

  • Keep account credentials and authentication information private.
  • Avoid sharing access to their GETHU account.
  • Keep their device and operating system updated.
  • Use reasonable security measures on the device used to access GETHU.
  • Report suspected unauthorized access or suspicious activity.

10. Security Incidents

GETHU maintains processes intended to identify, investigate, contain, and respond to security incidents affecting the service.

Where applicable, we may take steps to investigate the incident, protect affected systems, and provide notifications required by applicable law.

If you believe your GETHU account has been compromised or you have identified a security concern, please contact us at support@gethu.app .

11. Your Data Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights relating to your personal information.

These may include rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or other forms of control over your information.

Requests can be submitted to support@gethu.app .

GETHU may need to verify your identity before processing certain requests in order to protect your account and information.

12. Children's Information

GETHU is not intended for children who are not permitted to use the service under applicable law.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable legal requirements.

If you believe that information relating to a child has been provided to GETHU improperly, please contact us at support@gethu.app .

13. Data Location and International Processing

Depending on the systems and service providers used by GETHU, information may be processed or stored in locations different from the country where you live.

Where applicable, GETHU takes reasonable steps intended to ensure that information is handled according to relevant contractual, security, and legal requirements.

14. Changes to This Data Protection Information

GETHU may update this page as our systems, services, security practices, or legal requirements change.

When we make significant changes, we will update the date shown at the top of this page and may provide additional notice where appropriate.
