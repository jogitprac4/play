GETHU Data Protection

Last updated: August 20, 2026

This page provides a concise overview of how GETHU approaches the protection and responsible handling of user information.

Data Minimization

GETHU aims to collect and process information that is reasonably necessary to provide, secure, and improve the application's features.

Access Controls

Access to user information is controlled through authentication, authorization, service-level controls, and operational safeguards appropriate to the information being handled.

Encryption and Transport Security

GETHU uses industry-standard secure transport mechanisms for supported services. Sensitive information is protected through appropriate technical controls based on the system in which it is processed.

Service Providers

GETHU may use trusted third-party providers for infrastructure and application services. Where applicable, information shared with providers is limited to what is necessary for the relevant service.

Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only as reasonably necessary for service operation, security, legal obligations, dispute resolution, and enforcement of applicable terms. Users may request account deletion as described on our Account Deletion page.

Privacy

For the complete description of personal-data practices, please see the GETHU Privacy Policy.

Contact

For data-protection questions, contact support@gethu.app.
