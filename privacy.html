GETHU Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

GETHU ("GETHU", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, and shared when you use the GETHU mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use GETHU, we may collect:

  • Account information: mobile number, email address, authentication information, and account identifiers.
  • Profile information: name, profile details, profile picture, and other information you choose to add to your profile.
  • Content and media: photos, videos, and other content you choose to upload or share through the app.
  • Social activity: follows, follow requests, accepted or rejected requests, and related activity.
  • Communication and call information: information required to establish and operate audio/video calls, including call type and call status.
  • Device and technical information: device information, application information, IP address, logs, and technical data needed to operate and secure the service.
  • Notification information: device or notification identifiers needed to deliver notifications.

2. How We Use Information

  • Create and manage your GETHU account.
  • Authenticate users and protect accounts from unauthorized access.
  • Provide profiles, social features, searches, follows, and other app functionality.
  • Store and display content that you choose to upload.
  • Enable audio and video calling.
  • Send service-related notifications.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, secure, and improve GETHU.
  • Detect, prevent, and investigate fraud, abuse, and security incidents.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share information when reasonably necessary to operate GETHU, including with service providers that support hosting, storage, authentication, media processing, notifications, communications, security, or other technical services.

We may also disclose information when required by law, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, security, or property of GETHU, our users, or others.

4. User-Visible Information

Information and content that you choose to make available through your GETHU profile or social features may be visible to other users according to the functionality and privacy controls provided by the app.

5. Audio and Video Calls

GETHU may use real-time communication technology to provide audio and video calls. Technical information required to establish, maintain, secure, and terminate calls may be processed as part of providing the calling service.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

7. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide GETHU, maintain account and service records, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and protect the service.

8. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including requesting access, correction, deletion, or other forms of control over your information.

You may also stop using GETHU and request deletion of your account or applicable personal information by contacting us.

9. Children's Privacy

GETHU is not intended for children who are not permitted to use the service under applicable law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable legal requirements.

10. Third-Party Services

GETHU may rely on third-party service providers to operate parts of the application. These providers may process information only as necessary to provide the services for which they are engaged, subject to their applicable terms and privacy practices.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make changes, we will update the Last updated date and, where appropriate, provide additional notice through the app.
