Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

Privacy at a glance

GETHU respects your privacy. We collect and use information that is reasonably necessary to provide, secure, maintain, and improve the GETHU service.

  • We collect information needed to create and operate your GETHU account.
  • Information you add to your profile or choose to share through GETHU may be visible to other users depending on the feature and available privacy controls.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We may use service providers to operate infrastructure and specific GETHU features.
  • You can request deletion of your GETHU account subject to applicable legal and security requirements.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use GETHU, we may collect or otherwise process the following categories of information:

  • Account information: information required to create and authenticate your GETHU account, which may include your mobile number, email address where applicable, authentication information, and account identifiers.
  • Profile information: information you choose to provide for your GETHU profile, such as your name, profile details, profile picture, location-related information where provided, and other profile information.
  • Photos and media: photos, videos, and other media or content that you choose to upload, submit, or share through GETHU features.
  • Social activity: information relating to interactions and social features, such as follows, follow requests, accepted or rejected requests, and related activity.
  • Call information: information required to establish and operate supported audio and video calls, which may include call type, call status, participants, timestamps, and technical information required for the calling service.
  • Device and technical information: information such as device characteristics, operating system, application version, IP address, network information, logs, timestamps, and technical information needed to operate, troubleshoot, and secure the service.
  • Notification information: device or notification identifiers and related technical information needed to deliver service notifications.
  • Support information: information you provide when you contact GETHU, report a problem, submit a safety report, or otherwise communicate with our support team.

2. How We Use Information

We use information for purposes connected with operating and improving GETHU, including:

  • Creating and managing GETHU accounts.
  • Authenticating users and protecting accounts from unauthorized access.
  • Providing profiles, discovery, search, following, social activity, and other application features.
  • Storing and displaying content that users choose to upload or share.
  • Enabling supported audio and video calling functionality.
  • Delivering service-related notifications.
  • Operating and maintaining the GETHU platform.
  • Troubleshooting technical problems and improving reliability.
  • Detecting, preventing, and investigating fraud, abuse, spam, unauthorized access, and security incidents.
  • Enforcing our Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations and responding to lawful requests.

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share or allow access to information when reasonably necessary to operate GETHU or provide a feature you request.

This may include service providers that support functions such as hosting, databases, storage, authentication, media handling, notifications, communications, security, infrastructure, or other technical services.

We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, legal process, or lawful governmental requests, or when necessary to protect the rights, safety, security, or property of GETHU, our users, or others.

4. Information Visible to Other Users

GETHU contains social and profile features. Information and content that you choose to make available through your profile or other user-facing features may therefore be visible to other GETHU users.

The information visible to other users depends on the particular feature, the information you choose to provide, and the privacy controls available in the application.

You should consider carefully what information you choose to include in your public or user-visible profile.

5. Audio and Video Calls

GETHU provides audio and video calling features using real-time communication technology.

When you make or receive a call, technical information may be processed to establish the connection, exchange signaling information, maintain call state, troubleshoot technical problems, protect the service, and properly terminate the call.

Call-related information may include information such as the type and status of a call, participating accounts, timestamps, and technical connection information.

The specific handling and retention of call-related information is subject to the technical operation of the GETHU calling service and the retention practices described in this Privacy Policy.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, destruction, misuse, or loss.

Security measures may include access controls, authentication mechanisms, encryption where appropriate, monitoring, logging, service isolation, and other safeguards appropriate to the nature of the information and the service.

However, no method of electronic transmission, communication, or storage is completely secure. Therefore, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

Retention periods may depend on the type of information, why it was collected, how the information is used, whether the account remains active, and applicable legal or security requirements.

We may retain certain information for longer where reasonably necessary to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, prevent fraud or abuse, investigate security incidents, or protect the rights and safety of GETHU and its users.

8. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on applicable law and your location, you may have rights relating to your personal information.

These may include requesting access to, correction of, or deletion of certain personal information, or exercising other rights provided by applicable privacy law.

You can also manage information you choose to provide through the GETHU application and use available controls such as profile settings, notification settings, blocking, and reporting features.

If you want to delete your GETHU account, visit our Account Deletion page or use the account deletion option available within the GETHU application.

9. Children's Privacy

GETHU is not intended for children who are not permitted to use the service under applicable law.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable legal requirements.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to GETHU in circumstances where such collection is not permitted, please contact us so that we can review the matter and take appropriate action.

10. Third-Party Services

GETHU may rely on third-party service providers to operate particular parts of the application and infrastructure.

Depending on the feature, these providers may support services such as hosting, storage, authentication, notifications, communications, media processing, security, infrastructure, or other technical functionality.

Third-party providers may process information on behalf of GETHU only as necessary to provide the services for which they are engaged, subject to applicable agreements and privacy requirements.

Third-party services may also have their own privacy policies and terms. Where appropriate, additional information about relevant third-party services may be provided within the application or related documentation.

11. International Data Processing

Depending on where you use GETHU and where our service providers operate, your information may be processed in countries other than the country in which you live.

Where applicable, we take reasonable steps to ensure that international processing is subject to appropriate safeguards and applicable legal requirements.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to GETHU, our data practices, applicable law, or our services.

When we make material changes, we will update the Last updated date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, provide additional notice through the application or other suitable means.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, privacy-related concerns, or requests concerning your personal information, please contact GETHU.
