Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy. This policy explains what information GETHU collects, why we use it, how it is handled, and the choices available to you.

Last updated: August 21, 2026
In simple terms

GETHU collects information needed to create your account, provide the features you use, keep the platform secure, and improve the service.

Information you choose to add to your profile or share through GETHU may be visible to other users depending on the feature and available controls.

We do not sell your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide when you create or use your GETHU account, information generated through your use of the application, and technical information required to operate and secure the service.

Account information

This may include your mobile number, email address where applicable, authentication information, account identifiers, and information associated with your account.

Profile information

This includes information you choose to add to your profile, such as your name, profile details, profile picture, and other information you provide.

Photos, videos and other content

We process photos, videos, and other content that you choose to upload, submit, or share through GETHU.

Social activity

We process information related to GETHU's social features, including follows, follow requests, accepted or rejected requests, and related activity.

Call information

When you use audio or video calling, we process information required to establish and operate the call. This may include call type, call status, participants, timestamps, and technical connection information.

Device and technical information

We may collect information such as device characteristics, operating system, application version, IP address, network information, logs, timestamps, and other technical information needed to operate, troubleshoot, and secure GETHU.

Support information

If you contact us, report a problem, or submit a privacy or safety request, we may process the information you provide to handle your request.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to provide and protect GETHU, including to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Authenticate users and protect accounts from unauthorized access.
  • Provide profiles, discovery, search, following, and other social features.
  • Store and display content that you choose to share.
  • Enable audio and video calling.
  • Deliver service-related notifications.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, and improve GETHU.
  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, spam, unauthorized access, and security incidents.
  • Enforce our Terms of Service and Community Guidelines .
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share information with service providers that help us operate GETHU. These providers may support services such as hosting, storage, authentication, notifications, communications, media handling, security, or technical infrastructure.

We may also disclose information when required by applicable law or legal process, or when reasonably necessary to protect the rights, safety, security, or property of GETHU, our users, or others.

4. Information Visible to Other Users

GETHU includes profiles and social features. Information that you choose to add to your profile or share through user-facing features may therefore be visible to other GETHU users.

What other users can see depends on the feature, the information you provide, and the privacy controls available in GETHU.

Please consider carefully what information you choose to share through your profile and other user-visible features.

5. Audio and Video Calls

GETHU provides audio and video calling using real-time communication technology.

When you make or receive a call, technical information may be processed to establish the connection, exchange signaling information, maintain call state, troubleshoot technical problems, and terminate the call correctly.

Call-related information may include the call type, call status, participating accounts, timestamps, and technical connection information.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, misuse, loss, or destruction.

Security measures may include access controls, authentication, encryption where appropriate, monitoring, logging, and other safeguards appropriate to the information and the service.

No electronic transmission or storage system is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

For additional information, see our Security page.

7. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide GETHU and for legitimate security, operational, and legal purposes.

Retention depends on factors such as the type of information, how it is used, whether your account remains active, and applicable legal requirements.

Certain information may be retained after account deletion where necessary to comply with law, resolve disputes, prevent fraud or abuse, investigate security incidents, or protect the rights and safety of GETHU and its users.

8. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including the right to request access, correction, deletion, or other forms of control.

You can manage information you provide through the GETHU application and use available controls such as profile settings, notification settings, blocking, and reporting features.

You can also request deletion of your GETHU account. See our Account Deletion page for instructions.

9. Children's Privacy

GETHU is not intended for children who are not permitted to use the service under applicable law.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable legal requirements.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to GETHU in circumstances where such collection is not permitted, please contact us.

10. Third-Party Services

GETHU may use third-party providers to support particular parts of the application and its infrastructure.

Depending on the feature, these providers may support services such as hosting, storage, authentication, notifications, communications, media processing, security, or infrastructure.

Third-party services may have their own privacy policies and terms. Where appropriate, additional information about relevant services may be made available within GETHU or related documentation.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, data practices, or applicable legal requirements change.

When we make changes, we will update the Last updated date shown at the top of this page. Where appropriate, we may also provide additional notice through GETHU.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, have a privacy-related concern, or want to make a request regarding your personal information, please contact us.
