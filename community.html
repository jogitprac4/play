GETHU Community Guidelines

Last updated: August 20, 2026

GETHU is intended to be a respectful and useful community. These guidelines describe the behavior expected when using the application.

Be Respectful

Do not harass, threaten, bully, or intentionally target other users with abusive behavior.

Keep Content Appropriate

Do not upload or share content that is illegal, sexually exploitative, threatening, hateful, or otherwise prohibited by applicable law or GETHU rules.

Respect Others' Rights

Do not upload content that infringes another person's intellectual-property, privacy, publicity, or other legal rights.

No Abuse of the Platform

  • Do not spam or deliberately manipulate platform features.
  • Do not attempt to compromise accounts or systems.
  • Do not use automated activity to abuse or overload the service.
  • Do not impersonate people or organizations deceptively.

Reporting

If you encounter content or behavior that you believe violates these guidelines, contact GETHU support at support@gethu.app with enough information for us to investigate.

Enforcement

GETHU may remove content or restrict accounts when reasonably necessary to enforce these guidelines, protect users, maintain service integrity, or comply with law.
