Community Guidelines

GETHU is built for people to connect, discover, communicate, and share their skills and interests. These guidelines help keep GETHU respectful, safe, and welcoming for everyone.

Last updated: August 21, 2026
The GETHU community in simple terms

Treat people with respect, be honest about who you are, and use GETHU for genuine interaction.

Do not use GETHU to threaten, harass, exploit, deceive, or harm other people.

If you see something that violates these guidelines, report it so that we can review it.

1. Treat Others With Respect

GETHU brings people together. Differences in opinions, backgrounds, interests, skills, and personalities are expected, but everyone should be treated with basic respect.

Do not use GETHU to repeatedly target, intimidate, humiliate, threaten, or harass another person.

Healthy disagreement is allowed. Personal attacks, abusive behavior, and deliberate attempts to make another person feel unsafe are not.

2. Harassment and Bullying

We do not allow behavior intended to repeatedly disturb, intimidate, threaten, shame, or abuse another user.

Examples include:

  • Repeated unwanted contact after someone has indicated that they do not want further interaction.
  • Targeted insults, intimidation, or bullying.
  • Threats of violence or physical harm.
  • Encouraging other people to harass or attack a user.
  • Deliberately humiliating or degrading another person.

3. Hateful or Discriminatory Content

GETHU does not allow content or behavior that promotes hatred, hostility, or discrimination against people based on protected characteristics.

This includes encouraging violence, exclusion, or serious harm toward people because of who they are.

Content that expresses a personal opinion is not automatically prohibited, but hateful threats, targeted abuse, and calls for harm are not permitted.

4. Sexual and Exploitative Content

GETHU is not a place for sexual exploitation or sexually abusive behavior.

Users must not use GETHU to:

  • Share sexually explicit content where it is prohibited by GETHU or applicable law.
  • Sexually exploit, pressure, or coerce another person.
  • Request or distribute intimate content without consent.
  • Use another person's private or intimate material to threaten, embarrass, or control them.
  • Facilitate sexual exploitation or trafficking.
Consent matters

Never assume that someone has consented to receive, share, record, or redistribute intimate material.

5. Protecting Children and Young People

GETHU does not permit sexual exploitation or abuse of children under any circumstances.

Users must not create, upload, request, distribute, or facilitate sexual content involving minors.

Any attempt to exploit, groom, sexually contact, or otherwise abuse a child through GETHU is prohibited.

We may take immediate action when we identify behavior that presents a serious risk to a child or another person.

6. Violence and Threats

Do not use GETHU to threaten, encourage, organize, or celebrate serious violence against individuals or groups.

Content or behavior that creates a credible threat to another person's safety may result in immediate action on the account.

Legitimate discussion of news, history, education, or public events is different from using GETHU to encourage real-world violence.

7. Fraud, Deception, and Scams

GETHU must not be used to deceive people for financial, personal, or other unlawful gain.

Prohibited behavior includes:

  • Impersonating another person or organization in a deceptive manner.
  • Creating fake identities to defraud or manipulate users.
  • Running scams or fraudulent schemes.
  • Requesting money through deceptive claims.
  • Using GETHU to distribute phishing links or other deceptive requests for credentials or sensitive information.
  • Manipulating users through deliberately false information for personal gain.

8. Spam and Misuse

GETHU should be used for genuine interaction and participation rather than automated or abusive activity.

This includes:

  • Sending excessive unwanted requests or interactions.
  • Creating accounts primarily for spam.
  • Using automated systems to abuse GETHU features.
  • Repeatedly distributing unwanted promotional content.
  • Attempting to manipulate platform features, rankings, or engagement through artificial activity.

9. Authenticity and Accounts

Users are expected to provide truthful information where GETHU requires it and to use their accounts responsibly.

Do not impersonate another person, deliberately misrepresent your identity to deceive others, or create accounts for abusive purposes.

You are responsible for activity performed through your account and should take reasonable steps to keep your account secure.

10. Respect Other People's Privacy

Do not use GETHU to expose, publish, distribute, or misuse another person's private information without appropriate permission.

This includes personal contact information, private communications, credentials, intimate material, or other information that a person has not chosen to make publicly available.

If you believe your personal information has been misused, contact GETHU support.

For information about how GETHU handles personal information, see our Privacy Policy .

11. Responsible Use of Calls

GETHU audio and video calls are intended for genuine communication between users.

Do not use calls to threaten, harass, sexually exploit, scam, impersonate, or otherwise abuse another person.

Respect the other person's boundaries. If someone does not want to continue a call or interaction, respect their decision.

12. Illegal or Dangerous Activity

GETHU must not be used to facilitate unlawful activity or behavior that creates a serious risk of harm to people.

This includes using the platform to organize, facilitate, promote, or coordinate serious criminal activity.

We may take appropriate action when activity on GETHU presents a significant legal, safety, or security concern.

13. Reporting Problems

If you encounter content or behavior that you believe violates these Community Guidelines, please report it through the available reporting functionality in GETHU.

When making a report, provide enough information for us to understand the issue and review it appropriately.

Need help?

You can contact GETHU support if you cannot use an in-app reporting feature or need help with a safety-related concern.

Contact GETHU Support →

14. Blocking and Personal Boundaries

If you do not want to interact with another user, use the available blocking or other account controls where applicable.

Users should respect another person's decision to stop communicating or restrict interaction.

Attempts to bypass reasonable user controls in order to continue unwanted contact may be treated as harassment.

15. How We Enforce These Guidelines

We may review reports, investigate potential violations, and take action when we determine that behavior or content violates these Community Guidelines or creates a meaningful safety or security concern.

Depending on the circumstances, actions may include:

  • Removing or restricting content.
  • Limiting particular account features.
  • Issuing warnings.
  • Restricting an account temporarily.
  • Permanently suspending or removing an account.
  • Taking additional measures where required to protect users or comply with law.

The action taken may depend on the seriousness, frequency, and circumstances of the violation.

16. Questions or Appeals

If you believe an enforcement action was made in error, you may contact GETHU support and provide relevant information about your situation.

We will review requests where appropriate and take action consistent with our policies and applicable requirements.

Contact GETHU →

17. Changes to These Guidelines

We may update these Community Guidelines as GETHU evolves, new features are introduced, or our safety requirements change.

When we make significant changes, we will update the date shown at the top of this page and may provide additional notice through GETHU where appropriate.
